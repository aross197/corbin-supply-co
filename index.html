Legal E-Commerce Reseller Blueprint

Compliance guide for selling Carhartt, Under Armour & custom tech

1. The First Sale Doctrine (Reselling Physical Goods)

Under the First Sale Doctrine, once you legally purchase authentic trademarked products (e.g., Carhartt hoodies or Under Armour shirts), you have the legal right to resell those specific items without brand authorization.

2. Wholesale Accounts & Official Dealer Status

To buy brand products at wholesale prices (40-60% below retail), apply for official B2B dealer accounts with Carhartt Company Gear and Under Armour Corporate with your registered Business Tax ID / EIN.

3. FTC Affiliate Marketing Disclosures

If linking users to external official brand stores via referral links, disclose that you earn affiliate commissions on qualifying sales.
